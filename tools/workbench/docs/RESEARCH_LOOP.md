# Conduct a conversational research experiment

[English](RESEARCH_LOOP.md) | [日本語](RESEARCH_LOOP.ja.md)

The purpose is to turn coaching into a conditional hypothesis, test both supporting conditions and counterexamples, and preserve the evidence. Changed behavior alone is not improvement. Session records establish actual execution, human assessment and adoption status.

## Facilitation and handoffs

Research concerns how limited perception and memory support relational/affordance inference, goal-directed choice and revision after new observations. Generated scenes discriminate mechanisms; a coach's explanation alone does not measure human cognition.

The owner confirms scene meaning and supplies coaching, decisive cues and exceptions. Codex facilitates, prepares/presents scenes, separates original wording from interpretation, and records hypotheses, controls and outcomes. Do not stop the conversation by asking the owner to select CLI procedures.

1. State the current question, stage and one requested owner action. Label the display as a motor trial, hypothesis-forming layout comparison or model evaluation.
2. Confirm meaning before coaching; fix ambiguous teams, target or body direction. Save displayed inputs and normally ask one concrete question. Do not score control mistakes as cognitive decisions.
3. Briefly note new information → predicted relation → selection/continuation/cancellation conditions. Separate explicit coaching, assistant interpretation and unresolved points; do not repeat a full principle draft. Ask only about ambiguities that affect the next decision.
4. Choose framework review, a discriminating comparison, synthesis or tool repair under the question gate below. For a comparison, vary distance, orientation, motion or observation freshness; record fixed/changed quantities and the question before presenting. Never invent the owner's answer or acceptance.
5. Keep provisional principle synthesis separate from implementation. During discovery, record possible cognitive mechanisms and missing evidence without immediately changing code. At the later implementation stage, separate executable mechanisms from judgment-text candidates; unsupported mechanisms require code proposals. Weight changes, forced actions and successful rendering are not cognitive learning. Evaluate documents with identical observations in independent no-principles/previous/proposed arms.
6. State findings, remaining uncertainties and the next action with its reason; a new scene/question is not mandatory. Wait for the owner's independent target/version decisions when adopting. Scene counts and success counts alone do not end research.

Keep a private `work/research-checkpoint.md` checkpoint with question/stage, source snapshots and receipts, original coaching and interpretation, hypothesis/counterexamples, next scene/question and limitations. Preserve the distinction between forced motor previews and autonomous mechanism evaluation. For a configured Mac use [MAC_START](MAC_START.md); use its configured launcher and private paths. The generic commands below require the explicit `RPM_SESSION_DIR` set by the start guide, with an existing `work/` directory. For a new installation use [RESEARCH_START](RESEARCH_START.md).

## Framework first, then discriminating cases

Start/resume from private `work/research-checkpoint.md`, using [the public template](RESEARCH_CHECKPOINT.md). First map goals, relationships to maintain, breakdowns and repair across the scoped roles and phases. Reuse indexed coaching; ask only about gaps. Broad framework review is a valid next action, including when no scene has been shown. These organizing functions are hypotheses, not proven brain modules or a required serial processing architecture.

Before each detailed comparison, fill the template's question card: parent question, uncovered distinction, competing explanations with different predicted responses, fixed/changed conditions, capabilities, observations and branch stopping condition. If both explanations predict the same answer, redesign the comparison or return to the framework. Changing a numeric value without changing the relevant relationship does not establish a useful contrast.

Park a branch when it repeats an answered distinction, relies on a rejected premise, or adds detail that does not change the parent question. Save the conclusion, uncertainty, reason and reopening condition; select another meaningful gap. Do not force a new case after every answer. A question about the research method should update the method/checkpoint, not become a player's instruction.

## Stage gates and quality

| Stage | Work and evidence | Condition for moving forward |
|---|---|---|
| Framework and discovery | Goals, relations, breakdown/repair, role and information coverage; original coaching and uncertainties | A scoped gap and plausible alternative explanations are identified; do not require exhaustive rugby coverage |
| Discriminating comparisons and synthesis | Prior predictions, suitable dynamic stimuli, acquired inputs, counterexamples and conflicting reports | Evidence can distinguish the explanations; otherwise improve the task or leave the mechanism unresolved |
| Mechanism design | Explicit information/history/uncertainty/action operations, simpler competing models, matched motor capabilities | Reviewable proposals and a falsifiable evaluation plan; where parameters are fitted, test parameter/model recoverability with simulated data before interpreting fits |
| Interaction | Mutually responsive players with independent observations/memory; controlled perturbations and repair | Coordination is generated rather than supplied as formation coordinates; test shared constraints × local adjustment, with evaluator-only group measures |
| Human support and transfer | Actual human behavior over time, information interventions, unseen conditions and independent reproduction | Distinguish models using more than coached action labels; report the supported process and scope, not proof of identical neural implementation |

The executable model and standalone judgment document have independent proposals, evaluations and adoption decisions. Evaluate the latter with identical anonymous inputs in no-principles/previous/proposed arms. Neither stage progression nor a successful test authorizes adoption.

For coordination, examine who adjusts after another player's route, speed or role changes; whether duplicate/empty roles emerge; and how useful passing/defensive relationships recover. Scripted actors can isolate a cue in discovery, but cannot establish mutual adaptation. Shared tactics are known constraints, not access to current global positions. See [cognitive research](../../../docs/COGNITIVE_RESEARCH.md) and [implemented limits](../../../docs/LIMITATIONS.md).

Separate recommendations, participant reports and measured behavior. Available information does not establish attention or recall. Camera direction is not eye gaze; paused looking advances no simulation time and cannot establish human response timing. If a hypothesis needs unsupported measurement or movement, record that gap and defer that claim instead of treating a hypothetical result as evidence.

These gates are instructions for the collaborator and records for review; the runtime does not enforce research-method compliance. A new conversation must read the checkpoint and source records. Software tests cannot prove that an AI follows the method.

## Discovery before model implementation

Begin by collecting varied coaching and identifying provisional principles. Organizing related evidence is ongoing analysis, not a model-update cycle. Here learning means accumulating research evidence and revising hypotheses; saving coaching does not train AI weights or update the executable model. No AI API is embedded in the tool.

Broaden coverage beyond one carrier/pass pattern: carrier and off-ball support, defense and covering, phase changes, information availability and age, voice, shared tactical constraints and local coordination. Select cases to reveal new cues or competing explanations, rather than fill a scene quota. Keep an explicit coverage map so many similar examples are not mistaken for breadth.

Use perception, attention, remembered evidence, predicted relations, goals, action and reobservation as a provisional organization of the research. Record what was available, attended to or remembered only when supported by evidence; do not infer the coach's internal process from an action label alone. Preserve observation time, confidence, missing information, acceptable alternatives and switching conditions. Human cognitive/memory equivalence is a research question requiring separate theoretical and behavioral validation, not a result of coaching agreement.

- **After each response:** preserve exact coaching and source type immediately. For scene-linked evidence, save the matching acquired snapshot/interval and use `propose` for a pending draft with an empty player patch and unfinished principle fields. For verbal-only guidance, save the stated premise and original text in a private note with no observation reference; do not substitute an unrelated snapshot. Keep a short note of the new cue, contradiction and next action. Process discussions are not player coaching. Never postpone saving evidence until the batch ends.
- **During collection:** hold the model, principle and engine baselines fixed. Choose conditions that distinguish competing explanations or expose exceptions, rather than accumulating similar scenes. Record the changed stimulus before presenting it. A tool defect that changes evidence must be fixed promptly; preserve affected records and separate conditions across the change.
- **At a discovery checkpoint:** briefly consolidate new principles, conflicts and gaps when useful, then continue into an informative comparison or an underexplored role. There is no fixed number of cases that triggers implementation. A current-model mismatch is a recorded limitation, not an automatic reason to stop collection or tune the model. Repair tools promptly when they prevent valid observation or recording.
- **At synthesis:** read indexed evidence together and link provisional claims to source candidate IDs/revisions and snapshots. Preserve alternatives and exceptions instead of forcing one action per pattern. Keep original records unchanged. Detailed per-response formalization and automatic adoption are unnecessary.
- **At the later design/implementation stage:** assess whether evidence spans relevant roles and conditions, whether competing explanations and counterexamples have been examined, and whether cognitive/memory operations can be specified with inputs, outputs, uncertainty and falsifiable predictions. If not, select the missing evidence. Once this research stage is appropriate, prepare separate executable and judgment-document proposals and a comparison plan. Do not treat the existing runner's fields as the limits of the cognitive architecture. Unsupported mechanisms require a reviewable code proposal, not an invented patch operation.
- **At evaluation:** fix predictions, failure criteria and the proposed version, then compare before/after and relevant regressions. Coaching cases establish fit, not unseen transfer. Reserve and freeze unused conditions before their coaching/exposure under the [evaluation protocol](../../../evaluation/PROTOCOL.md). Run required engineering checks when implementation changes. Ask for independent owner adoption only after presenting the actual changes, results and limits.

Keep a private batch index with the question, fixed versions, evidence references, concise findings, unresolved points, synthesis status and next action. Full contexts and traces stay in private files; extract only relevant details for reasoning instead of repeatedly printing them. Avoid per-response rewrites, unchanged-model experiments and repeated successful checks. Verify the displayed stimulus when it changes or is ambiguous; do not require routine computer-use actions to facilitate research. These practices aim to reduce repeated token use; savings are not measured by scene count or claimed without usage evidence.

## 1. Capture coaching and acquired evidence

Look, play and pause in first person; capture through [presentation transport](PRESENTATION.md) or save manual feedback in the UI. Preserve scene/seed/time/player, body/head, observations/history, profile and both baseline versions. `context CANDIDATE_ID` exports player-available evidence without world truth. Clarify unclear application conditions and invariants; Codex may edit candidates but cannot make real adoption decisions for the owner.

## 2. Write conditions, exceptions, predictions and counterexamples

Perform this step at batch synthesis, not after every coaching response. The [synthetic example](../examples/conditional-coaching-edit.json) requests reobservation when holding the ball without fresh receiver evidence, except under an observed close threat. Fill `notes.rationale`, `expectedEffect` and `counterexample`; independently fill principle conditions, criteria, priority, exceptions, uncertainty and evidence.

```sh
npm run --silent bridge -- context CANDIDATE_ID > "$RPM_SESSION_DIR/work/context.json"
npm run bridge -- edit CANDIDATE_ID --file "$RPM_SESSION_DIR/work/edit.json" --revision CONFIRMED_REVISION
```

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `when.possession` | `any`, `has-ball`, `no-ball`: synthetic self possession |
| `when.evidence` | `no-fresh-receiver`, `fresh-receiver`, `stale-player`, `heard-call`, `missing-player-vision`, `observed-ball`: acquired evidence only |
| `when.unlessCloseThreat` | Skip under an observed close threat; does not prove unseen threats absent |
| `action` / `weight` | Add −2 to 2 to scan/hold/carry/pass/support scores before tactical mixing; cannot override eligibility |
| Optional `call` | `support-call` / `warning-call`, emitted only if the rule's action is selected |

`contextRules` holds at most 32 unique IDs; editing replaces the entire list. Retain existing rules and review overlapping additive weights. Strong preferences/tactical adherence can change results and must be tested. Calls are semantic events, not voice, teammate response or new self-hearing. There is no scene/seed/hidden-player/future-truth predicate. A new mechanism needs a code change, not an invented JSON patch.

## 3. Record before adoption

Run 1–20 steps of 0.2 seconds for the captured scene and eight fixed regressions, using the candidate's entire profile in both arms. Frozen sets selected before coaching add separate `frozenTrials`. Later observations may diverge after different actions.

```sh
npm run --silent bridge -- run CANDIDATE_ID --revision CONFIRMED_REVISION --steps 5 --mode preview > "$RPM_SESSION_DIR/work/experiment-preview.json"
npm run bridge -- experiments
npm run --silent bridge -- experiment EXPERIMENT_ID > "$RPM_SESSION_DIR/work/experiment.json"
```

Quick `preview` does not change session files. `run --mode preview` stores an experiment without changing adoption/active versions. It freezes candidate/revision/notes, initial input, both rule sets, judgment Markdown/amendments, traces and engine identity. Runtime/platform/architecture, Git commit/dirty status and lock/snapshot/result/document hashes accompany it. Snapshot/result SHA256 uses UTF-8 `JSON.stringify` of the saved object. Unavailable Git metadata is null. Viewing results does not recompute them; restart after source changes before recording.

The example expects carry → scan in the recorded and carrier-open conditions while retaining hold under pressure, pass with a fresh receiver and support without possession. All five constant-action controls and the old `scanBias:2` change fail this acceptance condition. This is a regression of the mechanism, not unseen validation. Real research requires separately chosen unused conditions, predictions and failure criteria before proposing.

## 4. Owner return and adoption

Return requires a reason. Once either target is reviewed the original candidate becomes immutable. `revise` creates a pending child with the same evidence/profile and current bases, retaining the parent ID. Recheck copied patches against new bases, especially whole-list rules.

```sh
# Only when the owner explicitly requests this review action:
npm run bridge -- return CANDIDATE_ID --target player --by owner --revision CONFIRMED_REVISION --reason "Reason for return"
npm run bridge -- revise CANDIDATE_ID --revision CONFIRMED_REVISION --reason "Revision plan"
```

The owner may adopt the player model while leaving principles pending. A revised principle ID replaces text in the active document while old versions retain old text. Revision conflicts reject with 409 and require renewed review; never fetch latest automatically for adoption. After adoption, use `run --mode active` and compare with preview. Synthetic owner flags do not approve actual research principles.

## 5. Evaluate judgment principles independently

```sh
npm run --silent bridge -- judgment-packet EXPERIMENT_ID --arm after --cases frozen > "$RPM_SESSION_DIR/work/judgment-after.json"
npm run --silent bridge -- judgment-evaluator-map EXPERIMENT_ID > "$RPM_SESSION_DIR/work/judgment-evaluator-only.json"
npm run --silent bridge -- export-judgment-json > "$RPM_SESSION_DIR/work/judgment-current.json"
npm run bridge -- judgment-review EXPERIMENT_ID --file "$RPM_SESSION_DIR/work/judgment-response.json"
```

Send only one arm's document/amendments and anonymous cases. Use `--arm none` or `before` for controls, `--cases regression` for known regressions. Keep labels, scene/seed, criteria, model outputs and world truth in the evaluator-only map. Structured amendments do not imply the entire baseline has been structured.

Use separate fresh conversations with identical observations and settings. Do not share other-arm answers, coaching, evaluator maps or executable traces. Follow [review protocol](../../../models/judgment/docs/REVIEW.md) for decisions, cited principles, uncertainty, counterexamples and self-review. Save responses through UI or JSON:

```json
{"arm":"after","group":"frozen","model":"Actual model/settings/date","output":"Full answer with case IDs","assessment":"Owner scores, unresolved points and counterexamples"}
```

Saving links a response; it does not certify correctness. Records remain `unverified-external-response` / `humanValidated:false`. Score compliance and rugby validity separately. No API/payment/submission is added; synthetic mocks are not real AI evaluation.

## 6. Restore and continue

Independent owner undo retains review history, parents, experiments and responses. After restart, restore the candidate scene to its body/head/history/profile with current approved rules. Saved old results remain unchanged; rerunning an old engine requires its recorded source version. Back up private state before long-term use and consider storage capacity. Record actual viewing, coaching meaning, conditions/counterexamples, independent reviews and restoration in private acceptance records; [Mac acceptance](../../../docs/verification/MAC_ACCEPTANCE.md) is the historical template/evidence boundary.

## Try forward from the captured point

`run CANDIDATE_ID --revision N --steps 7 --mode preview --start snapshot` starts at the candidate snapshot's position/time/acquired memory. Default `--start coaching` rewinds to the coaching start and stops at its end. Keep future trials distinct from historical revalidation.

The record uses `recordedInput.source: captured-snapshot` and `window: null`, retaining the original interval in the candidate. Use existing `view-request compare` for presentation. Temporary weights or forced-action assistance must be labeled in notes; they do not establish general principles or owner acceptance.
