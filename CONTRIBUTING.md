# Research and contribute

[English](CONTRIBUTING.md) | [日本語](CONTRIBUTING.ja.md)

Use public synthetic inputs in a new session. The workbench and CLI are local tools;
neither Codex nor a paid AI account is required. Begin with the README and the
[research start guide](./tools/workbench/docs/RESEARCH_START.md). Maintain its private checkpoint and follow the mandatory framework/question gates in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) and [RESEARCH_LOOP](tools/workbench/docs/RESEARCH_LOOP.md). The [manual tutorial](tools/workbench/docs/FIRST_RUN.md) is an optional software exercise. Its versioned proposal transport is inert data, with explicit local preview/reproduction and independent player model and judgment principles decisions.

Keep the player model (executable cognition) and judgment principles (independent documents) separate. A proposal
states its target, fixed base, conditions, predicted effect, counterexample,
protocol and evidence. Preview does not adopt. A local adoption is neither
scientific support nor acceptance into the shared artifact. The player model and judgment principles decisions and undo
remain independent. Use exact revisions and stop on conflicts.

Do not put private sessions, personal feedback, credentials or real-match assets
in an issue, PR or fixture. Only explicitly permitted synthetic world data belongs
in evaluator-only entries; never project it into a player-model or AI-subject input.
The procedural renderer has a bounded origin and separate provenance notices.

Open a reviewable issue or PR for a shared change. Maintainer review checks the
information boundary, independent player model and judgment principles meaning, negative cases, frozen references,
reproducibility and rights. There is no automatic publication or adoption from a
local session. Retain failed experiments and counterexamples when a hypothesis is
withdrawn. Record unperformed human or AI evaluations as unperformed.

## Licensing contributions

Submit only material you can license under the applicable scope in LICENSE-SCOPE.md. Authored contributions use MIT; retain provenance and third-party notices. Do not submit private sessions or real match/player data. No additional CLA or DCO is introduced.

## Scope and coherent changes

Follow [ADR-002](docs/decisions/ADR-002-research-boundaries.md) for directory ownership and contract changes. Settle the observation, action and record contracts before implementing a new mechanism. Separate software correctness, hypothesis evaluation and local player model and judgment principles adoption. Attack, defense and transition share cognition mechanisms; do not equate not holding the ball with defending.

Before a commit, inspect the exact staged diff and public file inventory. Keep private plans and feedback outside the repository. Prefer coherent commits with their relevant tests; consolidate unpublished fixups before review when appropriate. Preserve published history and frozen sources unless a specific migration or incident requires an explicitly authorized change. Run `npm run check`, and `npm run test:browser` for UI/bridge changes. New scene stimuli also need inspection of the rendered player view; a valid JSON file is not evidence of a plausible situation.

Update both language editions when behavior, commands or limitations change. Follow [naming and compatibility](docs/NAMING.md).
